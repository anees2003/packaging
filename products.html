Products

The small printed parts that finish a product.

Five routes, one plant. Below are the formats we run most often, with the specifications buyers ask for first.

Route 01

Offset printing

Sheet-fed offset for anything on paper or board: hang tags, size tickets, price cards, flashers, belly bands, folded sleeves, paper hangers and pharmaceutical cartons. Where a programme needs identical colour across hundreds of thousands of pieces, this is the route.

  • ColoursUp to 6 + coating, Pantone matched
  • SubstratesFSC board 190–400 gsm, recycled, kraft
  • FinishingFoil, emboss, spot UV, soft-touch, die-cut, eyelet
  • Typical run5,000 – 2,000,000 pieces
  • Lead time10–15 days after approval
Route 02

Printed fabric labels

Care instructions, size, fibre content, country of origin and brand marks printed onto textile tape. Soft enough to sit against skin, durable enough to survive the wash cycles a garment is rated for.

  • MaterialsSatin, taffeta, nylon, recycled polyester
  • ComplianceOEKO-TEX Standard 100 tested
  • FormatsStraight cut, centre fold, end fold, mitre, loop
  • FinishingUltrasonic or hot-knife cut, roll or bundle
  • Wash ratingTested to the stated care programme
Route 03

Thermal printing

Barcodes, QR codes, carton labels, pallet labels and shipping documents — anywhere each piece needs different data. Printed to the retailer's own label specification and verified before dispatch.

  • TechnologyThermal transfer and direct thermal
  • SymbologiesEAN/UPC, Code 128, ITF-14, GS1-128, QR
  • VerificationScan-grade checked per batch
  • MaterialsPaper, synthetic, freezer, tamper-evident
  • SupplyRoll, fanfold or sheeted
Route 04

Silk screen printing

For jobs where ink thickness is the point: woven and PU patches, decals, stickers, specialty tags and anything printed on a dark or awkward surface that offset ink cannot cover.

  • Best forOpaque whites, metallics, dark substrates
  • FinishesMatte, gloss, raised, high-density, glitter
  • SurfacesPaper, board, PU, PVC, synthetics, textile
  • ColoursSpot colours, Pantone matched
  • Typical usePatches, leather-look tabs, stickers
Route 05

Heat transfer labels

Tagless neck prints, care and size transfers, garment graphics and sports lettering, applied with heat directly onto the fabric. The comfortable answer when a sewn label is not wanted.

  • ApplicationsNeck labels, care transfers, graphics, numbers
  • FilmsMatte, gloss, soft-hand, stretch, reflective
  • ApplicationPress settings supplied per fabric
  • TestingWash, rub and stretch tested
  • SupplySheeted or roll, with application guide

Not sure which route a component belongs to?

Send the artwork or a photo of the existing part. We will come back with the route, the material and a price — or with a reason to do it differently.
