Technologies

The floor, department by department.

Nothing here is outsourced. A job enters as a file and leaves as a packed carton without crossing the gate.

Flow

Five departments, one corridor.

  1. Pre-press and colour

    File check, trapping, imposition, proofing and CTP plate output. Soft proofs go out the same day; contract proofs follow by courier when a programme needs them.

  2. Press

    Sheet-fed offset lines, screen tables, thermal print stations and transfer printing, each with inline density checks against the approved sample.

  3. Finishing

    Foiling, embossing, UV coating, lamination, die-cutting, eyeleting, folding, ultrasonic cutting and string attachment.

  4. Inspection

    In-process checks at every shift change plus AQL sampling at final. Barcode scan grades, colour readings and construction recorded per batch.

  5. Packing and dispatch

    Counted, bundled, labelled to the buyer's carton specification and staged for collection with full traceability back to the press run.
