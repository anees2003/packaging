Certifications

Certificates, with their scope attached.

A logo on a website proves nothing. Each entry below names the issuing body and what the certificate actually covers. Current certificates with numbers and expiry dates are sent with the first quotation, or on request.

SGS

ISO 9001 Quality Management

Covers the quality management system across printing, finishing and inspection at the Karachi plant.

Forest Stewardship Council

FSC Chain of Custody

Allows FSC-labelled paper and board products where the customer specifies certified stock.

Textile Exchange

Global Recycled Standard

Verifies recycled content claims in recycled polyester label tape and recycled board.

OEKO-TEX

Standard 100

Confirms tested label materials meet limits for substances harmful to human health.

Sedex

SMETA audited member

Covers labour standards, health and safety, environment and business ethics at the site.

Graphic Measures International

GMI colour certification

Print process qualified against GMI colour and reproduction requirements.

Retailer programme

Trim Supplier Qualification

Listed under a retailer-run qualification programme for nominated trim suppliers.
