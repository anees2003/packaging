Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Ali Print Pack (Pvt.) Ltd, E-94, S.I.T.E. Phase 2, Karachi 75500, Pakistan, is the controller of personal data submitted through this website. Contact: info@aliprintpack.com.

What we collect

Only what you send us: your name, company, email address, country, phone number, the description of your enquiry and any files you attach. We do not buy contact data or scrape it from third parties.

Why we use it

To prepare and send a quotation, to answer your enquiry, and to keep a record of commercial correspondence. For enquiries from the EU and UK, our lawful basis is taking steps at your request prior to entering into a contract, or our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to an order are deleted after 24 months. Records relating to orders are kept as long as commercial and tax law requires. Job applications are kept for six months unless you ask us to remove them sooner.

Who sees it

Our sales, estimating and production staff, and our email and hosting providers acting on our instructions. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to our use of it. Write to info@aliprintpack.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK, you may also complain to your national data protection authority.

Cookies

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. If analytics are added in future, this page will be updated and consent requested before any non-essential cookie is set.
