About

One site, from pre-press to packed carton.

Ali Printpack began in the late nineties as a small commercial printer in Karachi. Two decades later the same company runs a 75,000 sq ft integrated plant where artwork, plate-making, printing, finishing, inspection and packing all happen in one building.

Keeping every stage in-house shortens approval loops, keeps colour consistent between a sample and a 400,000-piece run, and means a delay in one department never travels across town to become someone else's problem.

Energy

Running the plant on waste nobody else wanted.

Pakistan produces roughly 50 million tons of agricultural residue every year. We press part of it into biomass pellets and burn it instead of coal, generate steam from recovered process waste, and run a 20 kW solar array on site.

The honest version: this does not make the plant carbon neutral. It cuts fuel cost, reduces coal dependence and lowers stack emissions against our own earlier baseline. Emission test reports are available on request.

How the energy mix works
  • Biomass pellets produced4,500 tons / month
  • Fuel cost vs coal baseline~70% lower
  • SOx and NOxSharply reduced, tested annually
  • Solar capacity on site20 kW
  • Steam sourceRecovered process waste
