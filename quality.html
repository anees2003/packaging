Quality

Quality is a procedure, not a promise.

Our rejection rate matters less than what happens when something is rejected. Here is how a job is checked, and what we do when it fails.

Control points

Six checks between file and carton.

  1. File and artwork

    Resolution, bleed, fonts, barcode quiet zones and spot colour definitions checked before plates are made. Issues go back with a marked-up proof, not a silent fix.

  2. Substrate intake

    Incoming board and tape checked for gsm, shade and certification paperwork against the purchase specification.

  3. Press start-up

    First sheet signed against the approved master under controlled lighting, with density and ΔE recorded.

  4. In-process

    Sampling at fixed intervals and at every shift change. Any drift stops the run rather than being averaged out.

  5. Finishing and construction

    Die registration, fold accuracy, cut quality, eyelet strength and transfer adhesion checked on sampled pieces.

  6. Final AQL inspection

    Sampling to the agreed AQL level with barcode scan grades and counts verified before packing.

When something goes wrong

A nonconformance is logged with the batch, the shift and the root cause. The customer hears from us before the shipment arrives, with options: rework, replace, or ship with a documented deviation if they prefer to decide.

Corrective actions are reviewed monthly, and repeat causes trigger a process change rather than another reminder to the operator.

Testing on site

  • Spectrophotometer colour measurement
  • Multi-source light booth (D65, TL84, UV, store light)
  • Barcode verification with scan grading
  • Rub, crock and adhesion testing
  • Wash testing for transfers and labels
  • Tensile and eyelet pull testing
  • GSM and thickness measurement
Innovation

What we are working on now.

Mono-material construction

Replacing mixed paper-and-plastic components with single-material builds that survive a paper recycling stream.

Adhesive reduction

Testing constructions that hold without adhesives that contaminate recovery, particularly on folded packaging.

Digital variable print

Shorter runs and per-item personalisation without the plate cost that makes small orders uneconomic.
