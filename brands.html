How it works

What a nominated trim programme looks like.

One approved master, every vendor

  1. Nomination

    The brand approves us as a trim source and issues the specification: materials, construction, artwork library, barcode rules and packing standard.

  2. Vendor ordering

    The brand's garment vendors order against that specification. We hold the approved masters so every vendor receives an identical component.

  3. Production and control

    Each order is produced, inspected and documented against the same master, with retained samples and batch records.

  4. Delivery and reporting

    Delivery to the vendor's factory with the paperwork the brand's audit requires — material certificates, test reports and traceability.

Already have a nominated supplier?

We are often added as a second source for capacity, lead time or regional coverage. Dual sourcing is routine and we are comfortable matching an existing master.
